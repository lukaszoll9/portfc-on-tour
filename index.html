The movement

One Club.
Every Corner.

Port FC is Bangkok's harbour club — born in Klong Toei in 1967, loved across Thailand. Now the expat community is taking it global. Stickers appear in hostels, bars, airports, and street walls worldwide. Each one links back here.

01

Spot a sticker

QR stickers are dropped at expat spots, fan bars, and travel hubs worldwide.

02

Scan & land here

One scan brings you to this page — the home base for the global Port FC community.

03

Post your sighting

Tag @portfc_tour on Instagram with your location. Every post gets reposted.

04

Spread the colours

Drop your own stickers wherever you travel. Orange and blue goes further every day.
