About the club

Port FC Bangkok.
Born in the harbour.

Port FC is one of Thailand's oldest and most beloved football clubs — founded in 1967 in the Klong Toei harbour district, Bangkok's working-class heartland. The club is officially run by the Port Authority of Thailand and plays home games at the legendary PAT Stadium, where the crowd is right on the pitch with no running track in between.

⚓

Klong Toei Harbour

Born in Bangkok's dock district — Thailand's biggest port and home to the club since day one.

🔥

Khlong Toei Army

The official fan group — drums, flares, banners and non-stop support every match day.

🏟️

PAT Stadium

No running track. Fans right on the pitch. One of the most intimidating grounds in Southeast Asia.

🏆

Thai League 1

Competing at the top of Thai football since the league began. Orange and blue through every season.
