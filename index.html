The movement

One Club.
Every Corner.

Port FC is Bangkok's oldest club — born in the harbour, loved across Thailand. Now the expat community is taking it global. Stickers appear in hostels, bars, airports, and city walls worldwide. Each one links back here. Each scan adds to the map.

01

Spot a sticker

QR stickers are dropped at expat spots, fan bars, and travel hubs worldwide by the community.

02

One scan brings you to this page — the home base for the global Port FC community.

03

04

Spread the colours

Drop your own stickers wherever you travel. Orange and blue goes further every day.

Sticker designs

The Stickers

Three formats — round, rectangular, and street-tag. All include a QR code linking to this page. Print them at any local shop and drop them wherever you go.

Round — 7cm
Landscape — 10×6cm
Street tag — 15×5cm
